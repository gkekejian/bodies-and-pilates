## The old design system (verified from the site's code and the captures)

- Colors: soft sage green #556450 (header band, buttons, footer accents) on warm off-white #FBF9F6. Dark sage buttons with cream text. Thin sage rules and outlines.
- Type: elegant high-contrast serif for display (The Seasons; Fraunces appears in the site CSS as the working serif), Libre Baskerville for serif body accents, Poppins ExtraLight and Montserrat for light sans text. Oversized serif page titles sit directly over photo banners (Pricing, FAQs, Book Your Session).
- Layout: sage header band with the round logo, right-aligned nav (Booking, Pricing, FAQs) and an outlined BOOK A SESSION button. Centered, narrow content columns. Generous whitespace. Small-caps letter-spaced serif section headings (INTRO OFFERS, CLASS PACKS). Prices centered in serif with a dark sage Book button under each. Reviews in italic serif, one at a time, lots of air around them. A photo-grid social section (Let's Be Friends, @bodiesandpilates). Footer is a simple "Get in Touch" block with icon rows: Mail, Location, Camera (Instagram), Phone.
- Photography: real studio photos everywhere, full-bleed banners and two-up photo rows, no stock, no graphics-heavy sections.
- Voice: first person plural, plain and warm, short paragraphs written by the owner. Hero: "Empower Your Essense" (the old site's own spelling). Section names are short and evocative: The Space, The Practice, The Classes, The Reviews, Let's Be Friends. Class descriptions are two or three plain sentences each.
